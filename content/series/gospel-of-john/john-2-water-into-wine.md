# John 2:1-11 | Water Into Wine

Passage: John 2:1-11

## Big Idea

Jesus reveals His glory through a sign that points to joyful abundance in the new covenant.

## Key Observations

- Jesus acts at the wedding when the need becomes impossible for others to meet.
- The sign reveals both power and timing.
- The disciples respond by believing in Him.

## Discussion Questions

1. What does this sign reveal about Jesus?
2. Why does John call this miracle a sign?
3. Where do we need to trust Christ's timing more fully?

## Cross References

- Isaiah 25:6
- Amos 9:13-14
- John 20:30-31

## Prayer

Lord, strengthen our faith as we see Your glory in the signs You have given.
