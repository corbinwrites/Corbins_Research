## Apply

1. Read your Bible everyday this week!
2. Memorize**[[Bible/ESV/James/Jas 4#1|James 4:1-10]]** 3.**Write a journal entry** (or several). Make sure that this journal entry is god-focused! Look to to the chapter for more ideas, but here are some ideas you can use for inspiration (you can even write on all of them):
   - Confess and repent of sin
   - Praise the Lord
   - Write your meditations on a passage of scripture (what do you learn about God or how you are becoming like Christ?)

## Questions

4. What was your favorite quote and why?
5. What was something you learned from bible reading this week?
6. Why is it important to record the works and ways of God in your life?
7. How might journaling aid you in doing what [[Bible/ESV/Romans/Rom 12#3|Romans 12:3]] tells us to do? In biblical meditation?
8. What do we learn about creating and preserving a spiritual heritage from these verses? [[Bible/ESV/Deuteronomy/Deut 6#4|Deut 6:4-7]]; [[Bible/ESV/2 Timothy/2 Tim 1#5|2 Tim 1:5]]
9. How is journaling related to self-accountability? Why is self-accountability important in our Christian walk?

Reflection Questions

10. How did Journaling go? What went well? What was difficult?
11. Do you feel this is a helpful discipline? Why or why not?
12. Will you do this again moving forward?
