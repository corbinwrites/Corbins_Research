Teaching: Livia

# Questions

1. What is your favorite quote and why?
2. Whitney says on pg. 225 that Christlikeness requires both fellowship and silence & solitude. Do you agree? Why or why not?
3. How did Jesus prioritize silence and solitude with his Father? Why was this a necessary part of His ministry? (Some reference passages: [[Bible/ESV/Mark/Mark 1#35|Mark 1:35-39]], [[Bible/ESV/Luke/Luke 4#42|Luke 4:42-44]], [[Bible/ESV/Luke/Luke 5#12|Luke 5:12-17]])
4. Whitney gives nine valuable reasons for silence and solitude - have you spent time in silence and solitude for any of these reasons? If yes - what was the circumstance and result of this time?
5. Do you currently prioritize silence and solitude with the Lord? If yes - how do you prioritize it? If not - why not?

## Reflection Questions on Silence and Solitude:

1. What did you do during these 30 minutes?

2. Was the silence and solitude comfortable or uncomfortable? Why?

3. Did your mind wander? If it did, what did it wander to?

4. What are some reflections/takeaways from these 30 minutes?
