# Gospel

Most people know about Jesus Christ and think he’s a good teacher (and he is!), but that’s not the end of it. Jesus is the solution. He is the way that we get to heaven and he’s the way that we can experience peace and joy no matter how difficult life is. But if he’s the solution, what is the problem? The problem is sin. Sin entered the world at the beginning of the world. God created the first two humans, Adam and Eve, and they chose to try to be like God and committed the first sin. We have come from Adam and Eve and we have sin in us, and it makes sense right? We sin around us in the world and in our own hearts as well. In my own heart, I’ve been angry with someone and wanted to get even. Or I’ve wanted to be a position of power and wanted to tear someone down to get it. That is all sin. My desiring to elevate myself over other people and even over God. However, I have been freed from that desire. It comes to my mind, but I don’t act on it. The reason I don’t is because I have my belief in Jesus that has freed me from sin. Those actions, sin, will lead us to Hell. A place where God punishes sin and since we have sin within us, we are punished. However, Jesus, removes the sin from us.

Jesus is able to remove our sin because he is God. He came down from Heaven (at Christmas) lived a life where he was fully human and fully God, and taught many good things on how to act in a way that pleases God. At the end of his life he was hanged on a cross and died. That death was the payment for our sin. He experienced death so that we would not be able to. However, he resurrected after his death. He came back to life! This shows that Jesus has the power over life and death. When we die, he will likewise bring us from death to Heaven, where we get to experience presence with him for eternity. Would you like to also place your belief in Jesus and receive this gift?
