# Card Questions

1. Favorite quote (or point) in the book
2. Describe your bible intake in your home and church while you were growing up. How has that impacted your view of bible intake
3. What does your bible intake look like today?

# Big Group Questions

4. Why is bible intake such an important part of knowing God and being godly?
5. Why can bible intake become a chore instead of a discipline of joy? In which ways might meditating on scripture deepen your appreciation of God’s word?
6. Do you agree with RC Sproul that laziness is the real reason Christian’s done’ study God’s Word enough? Why or why not?
7. Why is it important to continually receive Bible intake if such intake doesn’t emotional uplift you or give you a sense of peace each time?

# Small Group Questions

8. What can you do today to improve your intake of God’s Word in the areas of hearing, reading, and studying?
9. How did it go reading your bible this week?
   1. What was your experience this week reading the bible daily?
   2. What passage(s) did you read?
   3. What did you reflect on and learn about this week?
   4. What made it harder/easier to do this discipline this week?

      Be there every Sunday! Look at the example of these Korean believers and the reverence they hold God’s word. Let us not neglect to meet together (Hebrews 10:24-25) - [ ] The purpose of all methods of Bible study is to keep it - P. 24
- [ ] “Have you not read?” Implies a familiarity with the _entire_ Word of God. We must read it! - P.27
  - [ ] We should read every day! We can’t eat enough food for the next 6 months in one sitting. It must constantly be refreshed. P. 28
    - [ ] Find the time, plan, and meditate on it!
      - [ ] “What characteristic of God do you see in this passage?”
- [ ] Studying God’s Word - [ ] “Reading gives s breadth, but study gives us depth.”
      Want to grow in a particular way, study that concept. Want to be less angry? look up how the Bible talks about anger and learn what it says. Want to learn how to love God more? see where the word love is used and see what you learn. Want to be a leader? Study the life of Jesus and see how he led. - [ ] To study well though we must first, set our hearts! Set your heart to the task. Be excited about it! The willingness of the Bereans - P.21
- [ ] Don’t always expect to get an emotional charge or feeling of quiet peace while reading the Bible P.34
