# **Homework** 1. Memorize **[[Bible/ESV/James/Jas 4#1|James 4:1-6]]** 2.**Read your Bible every day** this week
3. **Evaluate how you are currently serving**:
   1. For those** who currently** serve in a ministry (or ministries) at City Light:
      1. Why do you serve in that ministry (or ministries)?
      2. Are there sacrifices you have to make to serve in your commitments, or is it something that you chose for ease/convenience?
      3. Do you serve in all your commitments with a joyful heart, reflecting all six motivations?
   2. For those who **do not currently** serve in a ministry at City Light: 4. Do you serve the church/church body in another way? 5. Have you thought about seeking out a ministry to serve in? 6. What obstacles do you think are keeping you from serving?

# **Questions**

4. What is your favorite quote and why?
5. Which of the 6 motivations for serving do you struggle the most with & why?
6. If God doesn’t need us to serve Him, and serving (on its own) can’t make us sufficiently righteous, why does He want us to serve Him?
7. Do you agree with Whitney that Godliness requires a disciplined balance between worship and serving? Why or why not?
8. What are some way that we can serve others (believers and unbelievers)?
9. How can our serving display the Gospel in action to unbelievers?
10. Looking at what 1 Samuel 12:24 says, what are some things that God has done in your life that give you a desire to serve Him?
11. God gives us all spiritual gifts to use for ministry. Reflecting on the book, what are some things you can do to discover the gift God has given you?
