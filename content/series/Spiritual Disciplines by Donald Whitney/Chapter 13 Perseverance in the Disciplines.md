Teaching: Livia, Jason

Main point - Pursuing godliness by practicing the Spiritual Disciplines in light of eternity

# Homework

Choose one Spiritual Discipline that you want to grow more in for the rest of the year.

### Reflection questions:

1. Why this discipline?
2. Practically, how can you grow more in this discipline?
3. How can you persevere when you struggle to keep going?
4. What is an encouraging verse that you can look to in your practice of this discipline?

I encourage you to note down others' chosen discipline(s) and ask them about it after small group ends to exhort one other!

# Questions

5. What was your favorite quote & why?
6. What is the role of the Holy Spirit in our pursuit of holiness?
7. What are reasons why the Spiritual Disciplines should not be practiced in isolation, but by active members in the body of Christ?
8. Pg. 299: "So we need to remember,” advises J. I. Packer, “that any idea of getting beyond conflict, outward or inward, in our pursuit of holiness in this world is an escapist dream that can only have disillusioning and demoralizing effects on us as waking experience daily disproves it. What we must realize, rather, is that any real holiness in us will be under hostile fire all the time, just as our Lord’s was.” Do you agree? Why or why not?
9. How can persevering in the spiritual disciplines help you persevere in various trials you might be facing?
10. What does it mean to practice the Spiritual Disciplines in light of eternity?
11. In what way(s) have you grown in your Spiritual Disciplines since starting this book?
