# Notes

## Memorization

### Benefits of Memorization

9. It supplies spiritual power. It is available for the the Holy Spirit to draw from - P.39
10. It strengthens your faith. In a moment of discouragement it can “reinforce a sagging faith” - P.40
11. It prepares us for witnessing and counseling.
12. It provides a means of God’s guidance. When we must think of what to do in a specific situation, verses we have committed to memory will be helpful!
13. It stimulates meditation. Those who memorize will have a sword in their armory. By sharpening this sword we must reflect on it.

### Ways to Memorize

14. Have a plan
15. Write out the verses. Say them out loud while you write them with a pencil or pen.
16. Draw picture reminders
17. Memorize word perfect
18. Find a method of accountability
19. Review and meditate every day. “We cannot effectively pursue holiness without God’s word stored in our hearts.” P. 45

## Meditation

Definition: Deep thinking on the truths and spiritual realities of scripture, or upon life from a scriptural perspective for the purpose of understanding, application and prayer.

Meditation is like soaking the tea bag in the hot water. Simply reading is just a small dip into the water.

_Joshua 1:8 _- True success is promised to those who meditate on God’s word. - P. 48

“Scripture saturates all parts of their conversation.” -P.48

“When we apply the bellows of mediation to the fire of God’s word we see more heat (passion for obedience) and light (insight and understanding). P. 49
