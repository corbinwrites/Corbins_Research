# Questions

1. What’s your favorite quote and why?
2. How did it go practicing prayer this week? What went well? What was hard?
3. How might you apply [[Bible/ESV/Colossians/Col 4#2|Colossians 4:2]] and 1Thessalonians 5:17 in the midst of your busy schedule?
4. Why should our prayer be governed by the truth of scripture rather than by our feelings?
5. In what ways does Satan try to sidetrack our prayers. Be specific!
