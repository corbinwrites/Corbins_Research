# Questions

1. Consider Ephesians 5:15-16. What does it mean when it says “the days are evil?” In many ways, even the world seems to recognize this reality. How has the world shaped our understanding of time well spent? And how might we as Christians be called to live and think differently in light of this and other biblical truths?
2. Does giving systematically conflict with being a cheerful giver (2 Cor 9:7) who gives out of love rather than duty alone? How do we ensure our regular giving remains an act of intentional worship?
3. Does good stewardship of time mean we will minimize our rest and maximize our works? How might Ecclesiastes 3:1 protect us from a burnout prone lifestyle?
4. Can I ever buy a luxury item (or get oat milk in my latte) in good conscience? How am I to balance that with giving sacrificially?

Application Questions:

“Resolved, that I will live so as I shall wish I had done when I come to die”

5. What encouraged you about how your current time/money is spent! What fruit/blessing has God graciously given you to enjoy through it?
6. What is one thing you were convicted to change in light of the reality that we will have to give an account for the time and money God has given us?
