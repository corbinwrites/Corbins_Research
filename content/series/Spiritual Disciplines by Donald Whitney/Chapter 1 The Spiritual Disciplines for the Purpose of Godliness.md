# Questions

1. What is your favorite quote? (Or if you would prefer, a point that stood out to you)
2. In the past, how have you felt about Spiritual Disciplines such as bible intake, prayer, worship, evangelism, and fasting? Be honest!
3. In chapter 1 some people are described as faithful to the church and demonstrate great enthusiasm for the things God, yet spiritually they “are a mile wide and an inch deep.” What are some positive and negative characteristics of these people? How does someone become like this? What does it mean that “they have dabbled in everything but disciplined themselves in nothing?” What would you need to do to ensure this never happened to you
4. Freedom “comes through the master of any discipline… the freedom of holiness is the freedom to do what God calls us through scripture to do and the freedom to express the character qualities of Christ through our own personality” (p. 17-18). In Which areas of your life have you experienced this freedom? If you haven’t experienced this freedom, what steps will you take to bring yourself closer to this freedom?

# Key Points

- [ ] “Discipline without direction is drudgery” We must reflect on what the final image is! - [ ] Page 2: Christ-likeness “not merely to wait for holiness, but to pursue”
      [[Bible/ESV/Romans/Rom 8#29|Romans 8:29]]
      [29] For those whom he foreknew he also predestined to be conformed to the image of his Son, in order that he might be the firstborn among many brothers. - [ ] Page 3: However our pursuit does not qualify us - [ ] Page 3: This will transform us to have a “holy hunger”
- [ ] Spiritual disciplines are both personal and interpersonal. _Personal_ focus of this book and is on your own. _Interpersonal_ is with the church. Both will flow into each other, but both are **required**.
- [ ] These are “activities not attitudes.” It is not good enough for us to desire to read our bible. We must perform the action of it.
- [ ] Furthermore, it is not enough for us to simply do the activity, but we must do the activity **for Godliness**. We must apply what we learn. [[Bible/ESV/James/Jas 1#22|James 1:22]] “But be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves.”
- [ ] Page 7: These are sufficient for knowing and experiencing God
- [ ] Page 8: Must be derived from the Gospel
  - [ ] Julian of Norwich → She had a bunch of visions then used that as the basis of her faith
- [ ] Page 9: “Dinner isn’t done until the dishes are clean” - Chuck Jewett Godliness is when the dishes are clean. We can easily memorize, journal, even fast without being more godly. That’s not the point!
- [ ] 3 ways God brings change: People, situations, spiritual disciplines
- [ ] The desire if from God; we must discipline ourselves for the task
- [ ] Disicplines are us getting on God’s path to see him (Zacheus)
- [ ] Don’t be a mile wide and inch deep?
- [ ] “You studied this a lot why not your bible?” - Rachelle. Everyone in this room is capable of discipline: College degrees, athletic accomplishments, business (business owners). God is greater than a degree, paycheck, a “win”
- [ ] To develop spiritual fruit, spiritual discipline!
- [ ] Freedom that comes from practice. Want to counsel, teach, encourage, serve → Spiritual disciplines!
- [ ] True Joy comes from here
