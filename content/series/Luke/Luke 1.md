# Luke 1

Passage: John 1:1-18

## Big Idea

Jesus is the eternal Word who reveals the Father and brings life to His people.

## Key Observations

- John begins with Christ before creation.
- Light and life frame the whole prologue.
- Grace and truth are fully revealed in Jesus.

## Discussion Questions

1. What does this passage teach about who Jesus is?
2. Why does John begin with eternity instead of Bethlehem?
3. How should this passage deepen our trust in Christ?

## Cross References

- Hebrews 1:1-4
- Colossians 1:15-20

## Prayer

Lord, help us see Christ clearly and worship Him as the eternal Word made flesh.
