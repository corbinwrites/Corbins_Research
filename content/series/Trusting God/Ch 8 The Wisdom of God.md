# Homework Question

1. We should focus not on the reasons for our difficulties but on the lessons we can learn from them. Read Deuteronomy 8:2-5.
   1. What lesson did the Israelites learn from their experience of getting only one day’s food at a time?
   2. Why do you think this lesson would be important for them later when they were prosperous in Canaan? (Read Deuteronomy 8:10-18.)

# Possible Devotional Scriptures

- [[Bible/ESV/Psalms/Ps 147#5|Psalm 147:5]]
- Jeremiah 10:12
- [[Bible/ESV/Romans/Rom 11#33|Romans 11:33-34]]

# Developing Trust

2. What is the ultimate purpose of all things according to [[Bible/ESV/Romans/Rom 11#36|Romans 11:36]] and 1 Corinthians 10:31?
   1. What does it mean to glorify God?
   2. In what ways do you think your present circumstances bring glory to God?
3. What is the logical connection between [[Bible/ESV/Romans/Rom 8#28|Romans 8:28]] and 8:29? (Notice the word for at the beginning of 8:29.)
4. As we read in 2 Samuel 24:10-14, David was more afraid of suffering adversity at the hands of men than from God directly. 3. Why do you think David felt this way? 4. When we are facing adversity from other people, what comfort do [[Bible/ESV/Proverbs/Prov 21#30|Proverbs 21:30]] and [[Bible/ESV/Romans/Rom 8#31|Romans 8:31]] offer us?
5. What would you say to a friend who is struggling with the question why in the face of difficult circumstances? 5. What kinds of things would you be careful not to say?

# Men & Women Questions

6. Are there any past or present events in your life that tempt you to doubt God’s wisdom? If so, what are they?
7. Do you think we can fully understand God’s reason for any particular event? Why or why not?
   1. What attitude is expressed in Job 42:1-3 and [[Bible/ESV/Psalms/Ps 131#1|Psalm 131:1]]?
   2. What happens when we let go of our questions and trust God?
   3. Is it hard for you to let go of your unanswered questions? Why or why not?
8. Our prayers often seem to go unanswered, yet God is still handling us with wisdom. He knows that the very adversity we long to have removed is His means to help us grow. Read Psalm 119:71. 4. Describe a time when adversity was not removed even though you or others prayed that it would be. 5. Did you experience any personal growth through that adversity? If so, what?

# Trust in Action

- Memorize Romans 11:33.
- Share with a friend something God has been teaching you through this study. Next time you hear of a disaster or tragedy, take time to pray for the people involved.
- Tell God about the situations in your life, past or present, that tempt you to doubt that He knows what He is doing. Ask Him to help you fully believe that His reasons are good, even though He hasn’t shown you what they are. Tell Him exactly how you feel about this situation; then meditate on [[Bible/ESV/Psalms/Ps 131|Psalm 131]].
