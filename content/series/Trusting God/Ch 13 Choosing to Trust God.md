# Group Devotional

Let’s focus on [[Bible/ESV/Psalms/Ps 56#3|Psalm 56:3-4]]

> When I am afraid,
> I put my trust in you.
> In God, whose word I praise,
> in God I trust; I shall not be afraid.
> What can flesh do to me?
> 56:3-4

What a confident statement from David. Yet in 1 Samuel… it seems like he doesn’t do that. I enjoy the perspective of a commentator here, “Verse 4 rehearses the right stance toward God, with an implicit reminder of why this stance is so right, so rational. The faithful “praise” God’s “word,” the revelation that served Israel as its foundation. This God is greater than all flesh.”

David, here, rehearses the right stance. Yet we have seen him fail. He wants those who sing these songs to not fall in the same trap as him.

Psalms, just like worship songs, can depict the _correct place_ that we _should be in_ though we may not be there.

For example, in the song the Stand it says the words, “I’ll stand with arms high and heart abandoned.” Sorry everyone, you may have sung those lyrics, but I have sang in worship with you and I have not seen your arms high. If we’re honest, sometimes

[[Bible/ESV/James/Jas 4#13|James 4:13-17]]

Whoever knows the right thing to do and fails to do it, for him it is sin.

# Developing Trust

2. Have you had an experience in which you chose to trust God despite your emotions? If so, what happened to your emotions after you decided to trust God?
   1. Why do you think emotions work that way?
3. Why is God worthy of your wholehearted trust? 2. Is it possible for us to be worthy of His trust? Explain.
4. What attitude did Job express in Job 23:8-10? 3. In times of distress we must be careful that our feelings do not mislead us. Do Job’s feelings contradict the truth of [[Bible/ESV/Hebrews/Heb 13#5|Hebrews 13:5]]? Why or why not?
5. Read 1 Peter 5:7. Is learning to trust God and cast our anxieties on Him something we get better at? If so, how? If not, why not?
6. Sometimes when we are anxious, there are things we need to do. Sometimes there is nothing we can do. Make a list of the things you are anxious about at this time in your life. Which things do you need to act on, and which things do you need to leave in the hands of the Lord?
7. Do you tend to forget to trust God when things are going well? Explain. 4. In what ways do we need to trust God in good times?
8. What contrast is made in [[Bible/ESV/Proverbs/Prov 18#10|Proverbs 18:10-11]]? 5. Do you get your sense of security from your achievements and possessions or from the Lord? How do your actions reflect this?
9. What does God promise us in [[Bible/ESV/Psalms/Ps 50#15|Psalm 50:15]]? 6. What do we need to do after God answers our call, according to this verse? 7. In what specific ways might you do this?
10. Are you more able to trust God now than when you began this study? 8. If so, what has helped you most? 9. What help do you still need?
11. How would you explain to an unbeliever why you choose to trust God?
