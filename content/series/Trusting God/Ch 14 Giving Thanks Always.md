## Men’s Devotional

_[[Bible/ESV/2 Corinthians/2 Cor 12#7|2 Cor 12:7-10]]_

Outline:

2. Paul’s Problem
3. God’s Response
4. Paul’s Response

### **Paul’s Problem (v. 7-8)**

- He has a thorn in the flesh and it makes it hard for him in life.
  - Note: Thorn can refer to a small needle on a rosebush or a stake one would drive into the ground for a tent to fly away. We don’t know the severity of his affliction but certainly that is not good.
- Who does the thorn _really_ come from?
  - This thorn comes from Satan, but ultimately from God. (Satan is just God’s Devil)
    - He asks for relief 3 times. God says no
  - He is aware of the purpose of this thorn!
    - To keep him from conceit.
    - The thorn pops the air in Paul’s big head

### **God’s Response ( v. 9)**

- What does it mean that God’s “Grace is Sufficient” for Paul?
  - Paul requires nothing more than God. Yes, God’s love, but God is sufficient for Paul’s needs
- His power is made perfect
  - God is able to exercise the fullness of his power in areas of weakness. It gives him further glory, it causes us to be more reliant on him. God supplies the power, Paul (and us) provide the weakness

### Paul’s Response (v. 9-10)

- Boast
  - It seems like he’ll boast in something he’s good in. Instead he boasts in his weakness _because_ he recognizes that it is an opprotunity for Christ’s power to overcome his weakness.
  - Builds his identity on where he is weak. “Competence is not where God’s power lies. Frailty is. Feebleness. For there God’s grace ignites. There God himself dwells.”
- Content (peaceful happiness)
  - weakness, insults, hardships, persecution and calamity
  - Why? For the sake of christ. Because of Christ’s sacrifice
- Strong
  - He is made strong only by being week

Questions:

5. Do you view “thorns” (trials, difficulties) as situations in which God keeps you from conceit? Do you view thorns as situations that make you better? Why or why not?
6. How can you boast in your weakness? How can you build your identity on where you are weak instead of where you are competent? “A: How are you still happy at a time like this? B: You know, I couldn’t think of an answer for weeks on end, but then as I read God’s word, it became clear to me on how to act?”
7. Are you content with weakness, insults, hardships, persecution and calamity? How can you grow to be in peaceful happiness in these times, just like Paul.

# Developing Trust

8. What does [[Bible/ESV/Romans/Rom 8#28|Romans 8:28]] say about God working in our lives?
   1. How does [[Bible/ESV/Romans/Rom 8#28|Romans 8:28]] help us fulfill 1 Thessalonians 5:18?
9. What was Job’s reaction to the loss of his children and property? Read Job 1:13-21. 2. What is worship? (A Bible dictionary might be helpful.) 3. What specifically helps you to enter into a spirit of worship?
10. Read 2 Corinthians 12:7. Why was Paul given a thorn in the flesh? 4. Has God ever used an adversity in your life to counteract your pride? If so, describe the situation. 5. Why does God cherish humility in us (see [[Bible/ESV/James/Jas 4#6|James 4:6]])? 6. What are major sources of pride in your life? 7. How can you change the areas where you are proud into areas of humility?
11. How does humility help us deal with mistreatment from other people? 8. Do you have a hard time forgiving others? Explain.
12. What sort of attitude is pleasing to God when we pray for deliverance from some adversity? 9. Is there ever a point at which we need to stop praying for deliverance? If so, how do we know when we have reached that point? If not, why not?
13. Of the six responses discussed in the chapter —thanksgiving, worship, humility, forgiveness, prayer, and a heart seeking only God’s glory—which one is the most difficult for you? Why is this so?
14. Look at [[Bible/ESV/Isaiah/Isa 42#8|Isaiah 42:8]]. How can your actions better show that glorifying God is your top priority?
15. What are the most important ideas you have gained from this study?
    # Trust in Action
    10. Write a prayer to God, expressing the ways you hope to trust Him more fully. If there is someone you need to forgive, or ask forgiveness from, make a point of doing it this week.
    11. Memorize 1 Thessalonians 5:18.
    12. Ask a friend to join you for a brief time of worship. You may want to include Scripture readings, prayer, singing, and silence. Discuss what helps each of you to break past the barriers in your heart to enter a time of true worship.
    13. Watch your attitude for one week. How often are you in a negative, complaining state of mind, and how often do you have an attitude of thanksgiving? Keep a record in your journal. Share your results with a friend.
