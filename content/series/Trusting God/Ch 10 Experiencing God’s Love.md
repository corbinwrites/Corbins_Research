# Homework Discusssion

David counsels himself

- Looking to his past
- Looking to the future
- Addressing the present

Despite the feelings, David persists in trust.

- David starts with his past
  - Past tense of trust in verse 5. “I have trusted.”
    - He was trusting God. So prior to these difficult events, David reminds himself that he has trusted God previously.
- David looks to his future
  - He reminds himself of the hope to come! His heart will (future tense) rejoice. He will (future tense) sing to the Lord.
  - David could be reminding himself that this difficult time will pass. He could be reminding himself of Heaven to come. Both can be great reminders to pull our gaze from the present to the future.
- David addresses his present situation
  - In verse 6, “I will” + “he has” = “now”
  - David reflects on the fact that in the future, he will look back on this event and see that the Lord has dealt bountifully with him.
    - A “bountiful” - abundant, generously. The cornucopia overflowing from the “horn of plenty” in Thanksgiving. When the Chipotle guy stacks the bowl extra high and gives you an extra scoop of meat for free.
- David counsels himself to a place that says, “This is the Lord being generous to me.”
  - Again, not something that we may arrive to in a single sitting, but the correct mindset when we encouter trial. This is what true trust in God looks like.
