### What’s your favorite quote?

> [!note]+ ## Group Homework Lesson
>
> > **[[Bible/ESV/Matthew/Matt 10#26|Matthew 10:26-33]]**
> > Have No Fear
> > [26] “So have no fear of them, for nothing is covered that will not be revealed, or hidden that will not be known. [27] What I tell you in the dark, say in the light, and what you hear whispered, proclaim on the housetops. [28] And do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul. Rather fear him who can destroy both soul and body in hell. [29] Are not two sparrows sold for a penny? And not one of them will fall to the ground apart from your Father. [30] But even the hairs of your head are all numbered. [31] Fear not, therefore; you are of more value than many sparrows. [32] So everyone who acknowledges me before men, I also will acknowledge before my Father who is in heaven, [33] but whoever denies me before men, I also will deny before my Father who is in heaven.
>
> Context: In Chapter 10 Jesus is sending his disciples out to evangelize to the Jews. While speaking to his disciples he instructs them on what to do (v. 5-15), warns them of what will happen (v. 16-24), arrives to this section of encouragement (v. 26-32).
>
> Jesus chooses to encourage the disciples, because the reality is scary!
>
> - V.17 - **Flog** you in synagogues
> - V.18 - **Dragged** before governors (lead to a court hearing - S _trong’s_)
> - V.22 - **Hated** for my name’s sake.
>
> With all of that fear in mind, Jesus gives a command in 28 before the encouragement in 29-31,
>
> > [28] And do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul. Rather fear him who can destroy both soul and body in hell.
>
> > [!question] ❓
> > Who can destroy kill the body but not the soul?
> > _Man (specifically those malgined with Beelzebul/Satan)_
>
> Who can destroy both soul and body in hell?
> \*God
>
> \*So then, who should we not fear? Who should we fear?
>
> What is Jesus saying here in verse 28?
> \*Don’t fear men, fear God.
>
> *What does it mean to “fear God?”
> *Respect\*
>
> Excellent! But. Uh-oh, what does Jesus say in verse 31.
>
> > [31] Fear not, therefore; you are of more value than many sparrows.
>
> > [!question] ❓
> > How do we fear, but not fear? How does fear and trust relate to one another?
>
> So we see the mighty power of the Lord and have fear at it. Like seeing a big bear or wolf walking down the trail, but Jesus does a 180 and says, “hey that huge bear? Yeah, he’s your buddy.”
>
> With this small statement, we still fear, respect, appreciate God, but we also _trust_ God. This power is restrained and not used on us.
>
> [[Bible/ESV/Psalms/Ps 40#3|Psalm 40:3]] says, “Many will see and fear and put their trust in the Lord.”
>
> [[Bible/ESV/Proverbs/Prov 2#5|Proverbs 2:5]] says, “if you call out for insight and raise your voice for understanding… then you will understand the fear of the Lord and find the knowledge of God”
>
> Trust of God can _start_ with fear, respect, appreciation for his power, for who he is. We see the Lord’s amazing display of power.

> [!note]+ ## Devotional - [[Bible/ESV/Acts/Acts 17#24|Acts 17:24-28]]
> Context: Paul has walked into Athens and is proclaiming Christ in the marketplace and synagogue. In his discussions he pulls the interest of some of the governing officials of the area who bring him to the Aeropagus, yes a physical location, but that’s the name of the council. So here Paul is standing before judges and he begins to evangelistically convince the council of Christ.
>
> In the passage selected by Jerry Bridges, Paul shares two points: who God is and Man’s response to God.
>
> First, Who God is:
>
> 3. God is preeminent (first)
>    1. “Made the world and everything in it” - Physically, first
>    2. “Lord of Heaven and Earth” - First in rank
> 4. God is powerful 3. “does not live in temples” - Temples cannot hold his power 4. “Not served by human hands” - He doesn’t _need_ people to do things for him, he can do anything he wants
> 5. God provides 5. “give to mankind life, breath, _everything!_” 6. “he made from one man every nation.” - He has built all nations, and all peoples
>
> Second, Man’s response:
>
> - Seek God (v.27)
>   - Of encouragement is the fact that God is not far from us!
>     - Paul quotes two extra-biblical sources here to make his point
>       - Epimenides of Crete
>       - Aratus’s poem “Phainomena”
>     - By bringing these two sources in he shows
>       - Ecclesiastes 3:11 is true, “He has put eternity into man’s heart, yet so that he cannot find out what God has done from the beginning to the end.”
>       - God is so near to us that even these two, non-biblical writer’s nail it. They have had a “touch of the divine in their writing” but our eyes must be opened to the reality.
>
> Application
>
> - First, let us praise God that he has let us find him! What a wonderful thing that we have salvation.
> - Second, let us reflect on the characteristics of God presented here: His preeminence, his power, his provision
>   - **Based on what we read this week what is God’s providence? What do we learn about God’s providence here?**
>     - Providence defined, “God’s providence is His constant care for and His absolute rule over all His creation for His own glory and the good of His people”
>   - **How does this relate to the concept of trusting God?**
