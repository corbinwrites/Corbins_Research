## Group Homework Passage

Read **[[Bible/ESV/Psalms/Ps 78#9|Psalm 78:9-22]]**.

## Group Developing Trust Questions

2. God desires that we learn to trust Him. What do you learn about trust from these verses?
   [[Bible/ESV/Psalms/Ps 32#10|Psalm 32:10]]
   [[Bible/ESV/Proverbs/Prov 3#5|Proverbs 3:5]]
   [[Bible/ESV/John/John 14#1|John 14:1]]
3. Do you think Christians struggle more with questions and uncertainties in the midst of adversity than nonbelievers? Why or why not?
4. According to [[Bible/ESV/Proverbs/Prov 27#1|Proverbs 27:1]], what should be our perspective on the future?
   1. How does this affect our need to trust God?

## Men’s/Women’s Developing Trust Questions

5. Do you have a harder time trusting God in the major trials of your life or in relatively minor things? Explain why you think this is the case.
6. What is the difference between trusting God and obeying Him?
   1. How are trusting God and obedience related?
   2. Is it easier for you to trust God or to be obedient? Explain why.
   3. Describe a situation in your life where obedience and trusting were both important.
   4. Describe a time when trusting God made it easier to be obedient.
7. According to [[Bible/ESV/Isaiah/Isa 55#9|Isaiah 55:9]] and [[Bible/ESV/Romans/Rom 11#33|Romans 11:33]], why is it important for us to trust God even when we don’t understand what He is doing?
8. What do these verses say about our privilege of knowing God?
   [[Bible/ESV/Psalms/Ps 9#10|Psalm 9:10]]
   Jeremiah 9:23-24
   [[Bible/ESV/Philippians/Phil 3#8|Philippians 3:8-11]] 5. Knowing God is more than simply knowing facts about Him. What does it mean to know God? 6. What are you doing to get to know God better? 7. Do you need to make or renew your commitments to these actions? 8. Write a prayer in which you express your desire to know God more intimately.

# Trust in Action

Choose one or more of these ideas to work on. Groups may want to allow time each week to share experiences from this section.

9. Keep a journal while studying this book. Record all the circumstances in which you see God’s control and guidance in your life. Beginning this log now will help you see concrete evidence for truths discussed in later chapters.
10. Memorize Psalm 32:10.
11. Make a commitment to memorize [[Bible/ESV/Romans/Rom 8#18|Romans 8:18-39]]. This may seem like an overwhelming task, but if the passage is broken down into small sections, it can be memorized fairly easily. The rewards of having this amazing passage memorized will be more than worth the effort.
12. Think of a relationship in which you have trusted another person. Write a letter to this person and share your thoughts from this lesson. Tell this person how much you value his or her trustworthiness.
13. Ask God for the opportunity to share something about His trustworthiness with an unbeliever. Watch for the opportunity and take advantage of it.
