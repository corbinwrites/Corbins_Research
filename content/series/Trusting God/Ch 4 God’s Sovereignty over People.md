## Homework Question

Read 2 Corinthians 8:16-17

- What kind of heart did God put into Titus? Why?

## Group Devotional

_[[Bible/ESV/2 Corinthians/2 Cor 8#16|2 Corinthians 8:16-17]]_

Context: Previously in Chapter 8 Paul is speaking about the generosity of the Macedonian churches. They were not only generous financially, but also generous in that that they were “giving” Titus to Corinth.

> [!question] ❓
> How did Titus feel about going to Corinth?
> _He loves and cares for Corinth
> He WANTs to go to Corinth_

While Titus was moving to minister to Corinth, he has a heart that is like Paul’s, (v.16, “same earnest care I have for you.”). That’s a strong statement! Paul cares so much for Corinth that he has written two letters that carefully instructed them and beat down the arguments of his opponents just so that church would know of Paul’s love for them. That same “earnest care” is within Titus as well. He will seek to teach and shepherd the congregation in Corinth.

Not only that but Titus is “going of his own accord.” He does not feel compelled by Paul to go, but rather he wants to go by his own violation.

> [!question] ❓
> Why is it important that Titus would like to go of his own accord?

- \*Elders should not be compelled (1 Peter 5:2)
- Speaks to his interest in going
- Speaks to how God’s hand is at work!\*

Titus’s desire is to be with this church which means he’s happy to serve them.

Finally, the most important part, God has put this heart into Titus. A strong testament to the subtle ways God works in our lives! Note that Titus, _says_ this is of his own accord. Here is the strange enigma of God’s sovereingty on full display. Certainly, God has placed this desire in Titus’ heart, yet Titus feels that it is his own accord. We must acknowledge that both are true in Titus’ life! So can this be the case in our life as well.

> [!question] ❓
> Can you think of an example of something that is a desire you have that the Lord has placed in your heart? What is it? Why do you think it’s there?

## Developing Trust Men/Women Questions

6. a. God can and does work in the minds and hearts of people to accomplish His will. How should that truth affect your attitude?
   b. How should that truth affect the way you pray about your job or other concerns?
7. Why should confidence in God’s sovereignty in the lives of people keep us from becoming resentful and bitter when we are treated unjustly or maliciously by others?
8. a. Describe a time when you were treated unfairly.
   b. How did you react?
   c. To what extent did your reaction show trust in God?
   d. Would you like your response to be different next time? If so, how?
   e. What was the end result of the situation?
   f. What good, if any, came from this situation?
9. What are some specific things you can pray for in your own life this week, knowing that God can work in people’s hearts?
10. a. Describe a time recently when you were affected by someone else’s decision or action.
    b. At the time, how did you feel about the situation?
    c. How do you feel now about the outcome of the situation?
    d. Does your study of God’s sovereignty in the hearts of people affect your perspective on what happened? How?
