2. **What kind of psalm is this?**

_Corporate Thanksgiving. Elements of Lament and Confidence_

3. **If known, what is the historical context for this psalm?**

_This features David in Exile which can appear in two parts of his life, when he is fleeing Saul in 1 Sam 22-25, and when he is fleeing his son Absalom in 2 Sam 16._

_My personal opinion is the former because the region he is in 2 Sam 16 is not actually the wilderness of Judah, but rather more likely Gad. In 2 Sam, David seems to be heading out west since he is going from Jerusalem to the Jordan and they later fight in the forest of Ephraim (to the North). John Collins concedes this position._

_Rachelle is of the opinion that it is later because the Psalm seemingly implies that David is the king in verse 11. J Mac holds firmly to this position._

_So what’s the right answer? Doesn’t matter (it’s probably is the second) So it’s on me for choosing an out of sequence Psalm from a historical standpoint.… The most important part is that we have to think of a time where David is in the wilderness._

4. **Observe: Who are the subjects within the psalm?**

_My = David, All = Israel, The LORD_

5. **What is the intent of this psalm? How would it be used?**

_It reads as a possible lament that quickly transitions to thanksgiving. This would be used to give thanks to the Lord in a time of hardship (where opponents seek to destroy the life of the psalmist)_

6. **How does singing this in corporate worship shape the hearts of the covenant people?**

_It allows them to give thanks! Reminds them of the Lord_

7. Look at the confidence he has in the lord. [[Bible/ESV/Romans/Rom 12#14|Romans 12:14-20]] reminds us that vengeance is the Lords and he can deliver us judgement, but still gives us instructions on how to deal with enemies in the here and now.

8. David’s soul thirsts for God. Does yours?
