# Small Group Member Description (2026-2027)

_If you're joining this group, this is the expectation set forth by City Light Bible Church._

1.  ﻿﻿﻿Our small group members have listened to Pastor Chris' sermon on Galatians 6:1-2 delivered on Aug. 23, 2026. The recording is available at citylightbible.org/sermons.
2.  ﻿﻿﻿Our small group members are committed to attending small group regularly from start (early September) to finish (late May/early June).
3.  ﻿﻿﻿Our small group members engage in life-on-life fellowship by loving, encouraging, admonishing, caring for, and carrying the burdens of the other members in the group. They understand that small group is a place to both build up and be built up in the faith, a place to both serve others and be served.
4.  ﻿﻿﻿Our small group members are committed to completing all assignments given by the small group leader.
5.  ﻿﻿﻿Our small group members are committed to City Light Bible Church. For the people who are new to our church and undecided about which local church to commit to, they are allowed to attend a small group a maximum of three times to help them decide. After attending a small group three times, they can either commit to City Light and stay in the small group or, if they remain undecided about church, they will stop attending the small group.
